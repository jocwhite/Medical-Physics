Practice Questions

Use collapsible questions for fast recall. Add more as you study.

Why does TG-51 use kQ?

Answer: kQ converts the chamber calibration coefficient from the calibration beam quality to the clinical beam quality. In practice, it accounts for chamber response changes with beam quality.

What does a picket fence test evaluate?

Answer: It checks MLC leaf positioning accuracy, usually by delivering narrow bands at multiple positions and looking for systematic or random leaf errors.

What is a TVL?

Answer: A tenth-value layer is the material thickness needed to reduce radiation intensity by a factor of 10. It is central to shielding calculations.
